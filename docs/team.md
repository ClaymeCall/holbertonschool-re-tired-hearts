Primary roles set the default owner for each area, but everyone can pick up work in any area when needed.

## Availability

Each member is available at least 09:00–16:30 on days worked during the sprint week. Additional sync calls are scheduled ad hoc on Discord whenever a blocker comes up outside that window.

## Working agreement

- **Communication**: Discord is the primary channel for async updates and voice syncs.
- **Daily check-in**: a short standup covering what's done, what's next, and any blockers.
- **PR review**: at least one teammate reviews and approves a PR before it's merged.
- **Decision process**: unresolved technical disagreements are settled by a majority vote among the three members.
